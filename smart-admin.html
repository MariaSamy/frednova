FredNova approach

Start with a real institutional problem. Demonstrate one complete outcome. Use a controlled pilot with success criteria. Implement carefully, train users and support the customer after go-live.

Commercial model

The blueprint recommends recurring annual licences or subscriptions supported by setup, training, maintenance and carefully scoped customisation. Source-code ownership remains with FredNova in ordinary customer contracts.

Readiness discipline

A functioning program is not automatically a commercial product. Customer value, functional quality, security, deployment, commercial controls, customer success and proof must all be evidenced before sale.
